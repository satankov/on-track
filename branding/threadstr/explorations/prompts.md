# Logo concept prompts

Preview concepts for the proposed names `trackstr` and `threadstr`. Created with the built-in image-generation tool. These are raster explorations; no product rename or app integration has been applied.

Direction: a calm, text-first wordmark with one playful typographic detail, drawing on the app's private project threads, system sans typography, and flat neutral palette. Each board shows both names on light and dark backgrounds. Fine features require optical refinement for small use before a final vector is prepared.

## loose-thread

### Initial generation

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: preview-only brand exploration board for a private, local personal project notebook. Users write notes, decisions, meeting records and progress in private project threads. The existing interface is compact, flat, neutral, with Inter/system sans typography.
Produce an exceptionally restrained, expertly kerned, vector-like typographic logo concept board. Landscape 3:2 composition. Flat solid light background #f3f5f7, lettering #17191c. Bottom 23% is a plain flat dark band #111417 with small white #f4f6f8 versions of both wordmarks. Large clear lowercase wordmarks in two spacious rows in the light area: first "trackstr", second "threadstr". Exact spelling: t-r-a-c-k-s-t-r and t-h-r-e-a-d-s-t-r. Do not add or omit letters. The wordmarks are the subject, with consistent type and one distinctive typographic modification. Work with contemporary medium/semibold sans lettering compatible with Inter, sturdy enough for a 16px sidebar header. Use ample whitespace, meticulous optical spacing, consistent weight.
Only include the specified small board title at the upper left, the two large wordmarks, and two smaller repeats in the dark band. No extra explanation, slogans, color swatches, mockups, watermarks or texture. Strict monochrome. No gradient, shadow, 3D, cartoon character, separate mascot, separate icon, stars, sparkles, frame, or decorative container. This is exploratory design, not a finished font.
Small board title: "A / LOOSE THREAD".
Direction: a quiet wordmark with a sly typographic flourish. The FINAL lowercase r remains clearly legible, but its right shoulder extends outward as one very thin thread, dips gently below the baseline, and curls back under ONLY the final two or three letters, ending in one subtle upturned hook. This creates an understated smirk and an actual loose thread coming from the lettering. One simple open curve, not a full-word underline, not a closed loop, not a knot, not calligraphy. All preceding letters are plain, readable, beautifully drawn sans serif. The flourish is small and intentional, not extravagant. The same terminal-r construction must appear in both names and small dark versions. The feeling is intelligent mischief in a calm writing tool.

### Contrast correction

Use case: logo-brand. Edit target: attached typographic logo board.
Correct ONLY background and contrast. Preserve the exact wording, letter shapes, sizes, placement, title and small bottom wordmarks of this board.
The entire TOP 77 PERCENT of the canvas MUST become PURE OPAQUE WHITE (#ffffff), including all four upper corners, all empty space around and between the two large wordmarks, and all letter counters. Large wordmarks and title in this white area must be SOLID BLACK (#000000), clear and crisp, like black ink on perfectly white paper.
The bottom 23 percent must be a single perfectly uniform flat black rectangle (#111417) with the existing two wordmarks in solid white. It is bounded by one straight horizontal edge.
Remove ALL smoky shading, gradients, vignettes, lighting, texture, transparency and haze. This is a graphic design sheet with two flat solid background regions, WHITE upper area and black footer. Crucial: the large words must be plainly and immediately readable in solid black on pure white, while preserving their designed typographic details. Exact words "trackstr" and "threadstr".
