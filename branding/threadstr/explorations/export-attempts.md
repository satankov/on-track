# Generative export attempts

These attempts used the built-in image-generation tool to prepare the selected c/O design for light/dark wordmarks and social avatars. They are provenance, not the final export method. Transparent exports had artifacts and the independently generated variants could drift. The user then explicitly authorized local image processing. The delivered kit uses the selected c/O source and `../build-assets.py` instead; none of these generated drafts is an approved replacement for the selected geometry.

## wordmark-light

Use case: logo-brand. This is FINAL ASSET PREPARATION, not a new logo concept. Use the attached selected "threadstr" wordmark as the authoritative geometry reference. Preserve its current black-stroke weight, exact lowercase glyph shapes, proportions, optical spacing and fine ribbon separator gaps. Do not make the font lighter, do not add or remove crossings, and do not redraw the design into an ordinary font. Flat hard-edged professional logo, no texture, no grain, no haze, no shadows, no gradient, no 3D, no caption, no watermark.
Create the full wordmark for use on LIGHT UI backgrounds. Exact text "threadstr", t-h-r-e-a-d-s-t-r. Letter fill pure black. Remove the white background completely: REAL TRANSPARENT ALPHA background, with transparent counters and transparent ribbon separator gaps, NOT white gaps. Preserve the fine separation lines. The whole wordmark must be centered and fully visible with a small even transparent safety margin. Use a wide horizontal canvas, ratio 3:1, with the wordmark filling about 92% of canvas width. A single logo only. No checkerboard drawn into pixels. Deliver genuine transparent PNG image.
